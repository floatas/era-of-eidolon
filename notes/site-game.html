When you start playing Era of Eidolon either by using the Trial Version or buying the Orumant Box you can create up to two heroes that start in the town of Orumant. These heroes can be used throughout the game even if/when you upgrade to the Dagonar and Ravarcil Boxes.

Each hero consists of a number of attributes that defines him or her. These will give you a general idea of just how powerful the hero is and includes Ability scores, Lifepoints, Armour Class, etc.

Your hero starts with a default setting but it is up to you to shape him or her during the game for your own liking. This can be done in many ways:

:: Choose which abilities to improve when you reach new levels - this will decide what type of hero you play
:: Choose one of the many head graphics to suit your type of hero
:: Write taunts to hurl at the opponents in the Arena to show them that you have the upper hand
:: Buy new weapons, skills, spells and armour to tool up your hero

The game also has a lot of features:
:: Fight against other players in the Arena where you can cast dangerous spells, place traps, use special skills, throw bombs, heal your hero and do lots of other fantastic things
:: Compete against all other players to be on the top of the ranking lists
:: Train your skills in the Arena against one of the 10 Gladiators in singleplayer mode
:: Team up with other players and form powerful clans that can fight for gold rewards in the clan contests
:: Communicate with other players via in-game mail
