Skills:

Introduction
The list of skills below is divided into the Town products (Orumant, Dagonar and Ravarcil) so that you can easily see where you can buy them. Skills are used for moving around and making attacks with weapons in the Arena.
ORUMANT
1 - Move (move)
Moving around in the Arena is quite simple and you should have no trouble doing this...just beware of the traps that might have been placed by your foes!
2 - Wait (move)
Waiting can sometimes be a useful thing to do if you are not sure what your enemy is going to do. Use this tactical element to make your nasty attacks or spells come in at the exact right time.
3 - Panic (move)
Sometimes it is better to leave things to fate and hope that it works out okay. By deliberately working up a Panic, a skill tought be the masters of the Academies, you may increase your move speed. The downside is that you have no control over where you end up once your combination of jumps, rolls and sprints is over!
4 - Tiptoe (move)
Tiptoeing is useful when you must sneak home from a late visit to the local Tavern and don't want to wake up the wife. However, it may also save your life if you use the skill in the Arena because when Tiptoeing you don't trigger any traps.
5 - Disarm Trap (move)
Disarming a trap is only for those with the right skill...and this is it! Instead of just hoping that you will not step on a trap you can use this skill to completely remove a trap that has been planted. Note that you will first have to guess where the traps might be hidden.
6 - Zig-Zag (move)
When you move in Zig-Zag it will take you longer to get from A to B than when moving normally.however, you will be harder to hit with weapons for your foe.
7 - Dodge (defence)
Dodging is the primary defence skill taught in the Academies. It is simple and fast to learn but will only make you slightly harder to hit. This type of skill was invented by a long gone order of pacifists.
8 - Deflect (defence)
By using a weapon or your armour this skill allows you to deflect incoming attacks.
9 - Evade (defence)
With this skill you will be able to read your foes next move the second before he performs it. This means that you sometimes can move out of the way and take no damage at all making your opponent look silly.
10 - Slide (defence)
Sliding is a more advanced skill based on the same principles as Evading and Doging and it will make it very hard for opponents to hit you with weapons.
11 - Parry (defence)
The Parry skill is based on blocking attacks with your own weapon.
12 - Hit (close range)
Everyone who starts training in the Academies will learn to make this basic attack form with melee weapons. There is nothing fancy about the skill but it will do its job.
13 - Aimed Hit (close range)
With this skill the focus is on precision rather than speed. Use this form of attack if your opponent is hard to hit as it will improve your chance of winning.
14 - Whip Hit (close range)
The name of this skill does not reflect the weapon used but rather describes the speed with with it is performed. It is fast but not as accurate as an Aimed Hit.
15 - Place Trap (close range)
If you plan on using any of the deadly traps that are available in the world of Eidolon then this skill is a must. It allows you to place a trap in the field that you are in when you use the skill. Note that some other skills can be learned to overcome traps!
16 - Nerve Hit (close range)
Many would argue that this somewhat shady form of attack should be banned in the Arenas but that has yet to happen. The skill is taught by former assassins that know how to target vital nerves for great effect!
17 - Power Hit (close range)
Sheer power is the secret of this skill! Considering the amount of time this skill takes to learn is itsn't that accurate. But don't worry, when you hit your foe they are sure to feel that you mean business!
18 - Shoot (long range)
Most commoners know how to use a bow and as such there is nothing impressive about the Shoot skill. It is nice to know, though!
19 - Snapshot (long range)
Basically, a Snapshot is a fast variant of the basic Shoot skill and it will allow you to increase your rate of fire while only sacrificing a little accuracy
20 - Toss Bomb (long range)
Tossing a bomb would be suicide if you didn't master this skill, especially considering how dangerous some of the bombs of Eidolon are even to the one using them!
21 - Snipe (long range)
Like an Aimed Hit the Snipe skill is based on accuracy. It is the pride of the famous huntsmen of Mystor and is only those who are deemed worthy are taught the skill.
22 - Double Shot (long range)
Long training is required to learn the special skill of firing two arrows at the same time. However, once learned you will quick discover the damage potential of the extra arrow that hits your opponent.
DAGONAR
1 - Back Away (move)
This is a more controlled form of Panic as you will only move away from the foe.
2 - Run (move)
Running allows you to move faster but you will be easier to affect with spells.
3 - Tumble (move)
Tumble allows you to move through enemy fields. But you can still not end in same field.
4 - Arrowshield (defence)
Great for avoiding arrows but not very clever to use in close combat!
5 - Meditate (defence)
While Meditating you will be easier to hit with weapons but will resist spells easier.
6 - Bomb Cover (defence)
If your foe keeps hurling bombs at you then use this skill and you will only take half damage.
7 - Counterstrike (defence)
If you avoid the foe's attack with this skill you will also make an automatic counter hit.
8 - Impair (close range)
Hitting the foe with this skill will reduce his chance of hitting you next time.
9 - Slow (close range)
The foe will move slower if you hit with this attack skill.
10 - Whirlwind (close range)
This attack will cause a minimum of damage but it's very fast indeed!
11 - Rage Hit (close range)
You won't hit that well with this skill but at least you will make 2 fast attacks in a row.
12 - Berserk (close range)
Forget about defence! This is an all out attack skill and you will go right for your foe in melee.
13 - Demolish (close range)
Are you annoyed by the obstacles? Then reduce their size or completely remove the small ones!
14 - Blindfight (close range)
You won't hit that often with this skill but you will even be able to hit Invisible foes.
15 - Head Blow (close range)
If this attack hits it will cause a lot of damage and the foe's spells may actually fizzle!
16 - Pierce Hit (close range)
With this skill you don't have to worry about foes in big suits of armour.
17 - Arm Shot (long range)
Shoot the foe in the arm and his next attack will have a hard time hitting you.
18 - Leg Shot (long range)
With an arrow in the leg your foe won't move as far as normal.
19 - Turbo Shot (long range)
Yes, you will be able to shoot very fast but you won't cause much damage.
20 - Arrow Burst (long range)
Two arrows are fired in a row but the chance of hitting is rather low.
21 - Power Shot (long range)
This type of shot may be slow… but boy does it hurt!
22 - True Shot (long range)
This type of shot may even target invisible foes.
23 - Fizzle Shot (long range)
This shot may cause the foe's next spell to fizzle but it has a low chance of hitting.
