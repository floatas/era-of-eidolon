Armour:

ORUMANT
1 - Scrapsuit
Most people would laugh at the notion that a Scrapsuit should be regarded as a suit of armour. Basically, the suit is made from all sorts of waste materials like pieces of leather or wood - only few contain any metal pieces. A Scrapsuit is not very durable since the different types of materials are difficult to fit together. Many a wearer has suddenly found himself without armour as a Scrapsuit fell apart due to a blow from the enemy’s weapon and as a result the armour is only used by those who cannot afford better.
2 - Snail Shell
Slightly better than a Scrapsuit is the Snail Shell that is made of large pieces of shell that is taken from lake slugs. The armour is rather cumbersome and offers only little room for delicate manoeuvring, but on the other hand it is very cheap.
3 - Iron Shell
The Iron Shell body armour is based on the same principles as Snail Shell armour. However, the skilled smiths of Orumant have discovered that by using iron instead of slug shells they can make the armour fit the wearer better.
4 - Doom Suit
Doom Suits consists of breast and shoulder plates of metal and earned the name because the guards of the Arenas, Doom Guards, favour this type of armour. The suits are mass produced all over the country and are relatively cheap considering the protection they afford.
5 - Spike Suit
Spike Suits can be quite a nasty experience to the wearer as the many long spikes may cause harm if a wrong move is made. However, many brave warriors have learned to live with this since the mere appearance of the armour may discourage enemies from attacking.
6 - Blood Armour
These rare suits of armour are surrounded by mystery and are banned in many places because they are thought to be linked to some sort of evil. Blood Armour is made from an unknown, blood red material and even today no one knows who made them.
7 - Power Mail
Many famous heroes swear to this type of armour. Power Mail is strong, durable and allows for reasonable movement during combat. It consists of many small plates of metal that are fitted together in overlaying layers. The metal used varies from region to region but the smiths of Blissmor are probably the best at making Power Mail.
8 - Bull Armour
Bull Armour is fashioned from thick metal plate and comes with a great helmet shaped like the head of a bull, hence the name. Skilled warriors have learned to use the big horns on the helmet as brute weapons in the wars against intruders.
DAGONAR
1 - Trollskin
Hides from forest trolls are quite useful for making armour as they are very tough. Getting the hides for the armour is just as tough, however, as troll rarely want to part with their skin!
2 - Ice Armour
The name is quite misleading as Ice Armour isn't made from ice but cold steel.
3 - Lava Plate
The hardened lava of Vulcor volcanos can be made into plates of hard armour.
4 - Black mail
We are not talking about crime now but a suit of black armour worn by many warriors.
5 - Paladin Suit
Bright and shiny this is the suit of armour for those that need to think about their image.
6 - Chaos Plate
The twisted metal and strange shape of these suits have earned them their name.
7 - Vulcor suit
Heavy armour is not seen often in the region of Vulcor as it is simply too hot to wear in the relentless wastes of the desert. A Vulcor Suit is one of the few that are popular, though.
8 - Wraith Mail
The light material used for these suits makes the armour look transparent.
9 - Holy Plate
Suits of Holy Plate are highly prized by Heroes as they are blessed by gods.
