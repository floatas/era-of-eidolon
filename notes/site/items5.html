Gladiators:

Introduction
The three Queens of Eidolon have declared that any person must start training in the Doomsphere when he or she becomes 15 years old. A certain amount of time must be spent training each day, depending on age and natural talent and this usually varies from 1-2 hours daily. However, some spend all day in the great Arena, not because they want to but because they have to. These are the Gladiators who are all former criminals that have been caught and sentenced to serve as training partners...some for a month, some for life!
The Gimp
To ensure that even the youngest boys and girls that start their training in the Arena have someone to train with the wizards of Orumant created this being, the Gimp. He, or 'it' as most would call the being, is a simple golem that has been commanded to do certain things: shout helpful advice, make simple attacks...and lose horribly!
ORUMANT
1 - Scarboy
Scarboy is little more than a boy, and is thus the youngest of the town's gladiators. Scarboy is not exactly a natural gladiator and he often loses his fights in a horrible manner. As a result his entire body is covered by hideous scars that he has earned in the many fights. It has become almost a tradition for spectators to bet on what part of his body will be severed first in a fight. Needless to say, Scarboy can't wait till his sentence has been served so that he no longer has to endure this cruel punishment that doesn't really correspond to his crime of begging.
2 - Eliana
Eliana is the only female gladiator of Orumant. Her crime is considered one of the worst in Eidolon; she tried to hide the birth of her boy so that he wouldn't have to go to the arena for training. Ironically this is where she now serves as a gladiator herself and she has even trained her own boy some years ago! Many an opponent has made the mistake of underestimating her because she is a woman.
3 - Swifter
Swifter is only 5' tall but what he lacks in volume he makes up for in speed and agility. These traits served him well as a cat burglar but now he makes use of them in the arena. You could say that Swifter is somewhat dense for the reason he is a gladiator is that he was caught trying to rob the Queen's Guard of Orumant. Everyone knows how heavily guarded that place is!
4 - Mr. Traps
As his name implies, this gladiator has a knack for setting up traps everywhere. This dangerous habit has already resulted in the loss of an arm and many wonder when he will blow the rest apart. The crime that this gladiator committed was to place a trap in a business rival's shop, blowing up several noble persons. Mr. Traps is 'trapped' in the arena for life.
5 - Blaster
Like his brother, Mr. Traps, Blaster is very fond of blowing up things. Blaster is not as subtle, though, preferring to hurl nasty bombs at his opponents instead of setting up tricky traps. Before he joined the ranks of gladiators, Blaster worked in one of the mountain mines as head of the explosives. However, one day he suddenly snapped and blew up the whole supply of explosives, which caused many of the mine tunnels to collapse. He is now paying for the crime by serving his time as a gladiator and by the looks of it he will never leave the arena!
6 - Rascalion
Born in the streets of Orumant, Rascalion has always known to take care of himself. Ultimately he was caught stealing bread at the market and as a punishment he must now fight as a gladiator in the arena. Unlike most of the other gladiators, Rascalion likes his new 'career' since he is no longer a nobody but has earned the respect of the people of the town.
7 - Conjuman
Conjuman is the oldest of the Orumant gladiators and he is reputed for his fondness for spells. He comes from one of the mountains of Blissmor and it is said that it was Queen Sorena herself that selected him for the position as a gladiator since he tried to magically steal one of the royal spell books.
8 - Fitorius
Fitorius is a strong and fast gladiator that has won many fights in the arena. His mighty Doomhammer is an heirloom from his father and you might say that it is the reason why he is now a gladiator. Fitorius used the weapon in a bar brawl and being very drunk he didn't even realise that he managed to kill several men before he was arrested by the town's guard.
9 - Stronghand
Nobody really knows that much about the mysterious Stronghand who is the most recent addition to the group of gladiators of Orumant. One of the things that most have learned, however, is that he's a formidable foe who hates anyone that comes in his way!
10 - Multinios
Multinios is currently the champion of the gladiators of Orumant. Multinios is very much a Jack of all trades and finding his weak spot isn't something that many will have a chance to do before he defeats them in the arena. His crime was to refuse enrolment in the army of Eidolon. He said that it goes against his beliefs to kill others! Somehow, though, Queen Sorena's wizards have managed to 'persuade' Multinios into fighting in the arena and he has certainly become a very skilled gladiator. However, when he fights he always says prayers for his opponents.
DAGONAR
1 - Hexman
Hexman likes spells and preferably lots of them. What a spell does is not so important, but that does not mean that he lacks the understanding of how to use them properly! A properly used Fireball can cause a lot of destruction…and can send a man to prison in a hurry.
2 - Gammicor
Gammicor is a warrior at heart and comes from the outer wastelands of Vulcor where such talents are necessary in order to survive. He is both fast and strong and is known for delivering deadly strikes within the first minute or two of a fight. A dispute over Clan territory resulted in Gammicor's crime of maiming a man.
3 - Luron
Luron is a crafty man that discovered that spells are excellent for augmenting a thief's skills when breaking into mansions and castles. However, suffering greatly from arrogance and self-confidence can bring down even the best thief, and this was also the case with Luron who is now serving as a Gladiator.
4 - Ratface
This Gladiator has earned his named due to the facial features that makes him resemble a rodent. His pronounced upper teeth also make him lisp and all in all he is quite unpleasant to be around. However, he is not to be underestimated as a fighter as he is very fast and agile. His style of fighting is mainly based around run-by attacks, which seem to work against most opponents. What his crime was, nobody really knows or cares...they are just happy that he is behind bars.
5 - Tarran
Tarran is a former wizard that mainly delved into the art of controlling the element of earth. His great powers were once highly sought by builders as it would allow them to work faster. Alas, even the best wizard can make mistakes and so did Tarran – at least if causing a major landslide and thus killing 500 villagers can be called a mistake.
6 - Ogrilar
'Ugly' and 'huge' is probably the easiest and best way to describe this Gladiator! Ogrilar is a typical example of how wrong it can go for someone who spends far too much time under the blazing sun of Vulcor. You could say that the role as Gladiator is perfect for him as his crime was to slaughter countless men and women in the small mountain villages north of Dagonar.
7 - Micella
Micella is currently the only female Gladiator from Vulcor, but nevertheless she is a dangerous adversary on the Arena floor. She has a reputation resisting most spells and for gutting her opponents. Actually, that was also how she murdered her late husband, whom she claimed to have beaten her on a daily basis.
8 - Sir Zircon
This Gladiator is a former noble but the titles he held were immediately stripped away from him when he was caught stealing from the royal coffers. His background has formed him into a quite versatile warrior and he is more or less a Jack of all trades much like Multinios.
9 - Wraular
Wizards' experiments sometimes go wrong - Wraular is the perfect example of this! He is half man, half beast and is covered in coarse hair from top to toe. His demeanour is also that of a primitive animal and he can only utter a few words. Wraular has not committed a crime as such but the authorities of Vulcor have deemed him too dangerous to run free.
10 - Father Silmo
Father Silmo, a former priest of Dagonar, is actually the father of Multinios who is the leading Gladiator from Orumant. As they are linked in blood so you could say they are in crime. When Multinios was arrested it was too much for Silmo who in a fit of anger 'accidentally' punched one of the royal guardsmen in the head.
