In case you didn't find your answer below, feel free to write an email to support@watagame.com. We will update the FAQ as soon as possible.

Q: How can i find out if the game works on my mobile?
A: To see a list of the mobiles currently supported, check here. In case your mobile isn't listed, you can sign up for a notification email when the game is ready for your mobile. Do that here.
Q: The game is terminating suddenly saying "Application error" or the like.
A: This issue is currently beeing fixed. When the issue has been fixed it will say so here, so check back soon!
Q: I've downloaded the game, but i cannot find it in the menu. Where did it go?
A: After installing the game, it can be found in the "Games" menu or in the "Applications" menu.
Q: I have fought many fights now, but I don't seem to gain anything (experience, gold, etc.) when I win... What am I doing wrong?
A: Maybe you have been fighting someone from your own clan! You don't gain anything when fighting other members because you are supposed to work together against the other clans.
Q: It seems to me that I gain less and less when I win fights. Why is that?
A: It's probably because you have been fighting against the same opponent many times during the last seven days. When you do so, you will gradually gain less for each fight.
Q: I have sent a game mail to someone else but I never got a reply and he says he didn't get it. What happened?
A: The maximum number of mails you can have in your inbox is 15. If you exceed this number you can't receive any new mails and such are simply deleted. So make sure to delete old mails from time to time!
Q: I'm a clan chief and I think it would be cool if I could send mails to all my clan members at the same time instead of sending to them individually. Is this possible?
A: Yes, it is! In fact this options is available to all members of a clan.
Q: How do I create a clan?
A: First of all you must be level 10. If you are so go to the website EraofEidolon.com, go to "My account" where you will find the "Create Clan" link.
Q: How do I accept a fight?
A: Simply press the "Call accept key" (the green phone key on Nokia phones).
Q: How do I get new friends?
A: If you know the name of another player you can add this person to your friend list. Go to the Tavern/Friends and choose "+friend".
Q: What are Blood Duels and can I join?
A: Blood Duels (BD) are contests for individuals that run on Wednesdays in weeks when there are no Clan Wars. BD run at 08:00-11:00 GMT and again at 18:00-21:00 GMT. To win you must earn the most respect within the time limit, and the winner receives a Magic Item!
Q: Who is the Mayor? Can I fight him?
A: The Mayor is the name of an administrator account that occationally sends out information mails and replies to mails that you may send to him from the Tavern. The Mayor does not accept or respond to challenges.
Q: Sometimes when I receive a mail it is full of ****. Why is that?
A: We have tried to make a profanity (bad language) filter, and every time someone tries to use a word that we have deemed unwanted in our game it will be replaced with asterixes.
Q: Is the Trial version of the game free?
A: Yes, you don't have to pay anything to get the Trial version of the game. You should note, however, that you will have to pay a fee to the tele company for the trafic you generate when gaming.
Q: How do I save my game before I exit it?
A: You don't have to save the game as the server does so automatically. Simply close the application and your data are saved.
