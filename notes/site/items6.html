Charms:

Introduction
Charms are magic items that will artificially boost Heroes wearing them. Charms can only be gained by winning the so-called Blood Duels.
1 - Force Stone
A Force Stone is made from the black stones that can be found deep beneath the earth. These are ideal for the enchantments that are used to create the effect that increases the wearer's Strength Ability score by +3.
2 - Hunter Gem
Hunter Gems are simple gemstones that are kept in their rough form. They are highly prized by hunters and archers as they improve the wearer's Dexterity Ability score by +3.
3 - Mage Ring
These beautiful and thick rings are made by mages for mages. They are wrought in cold silver and bestows a +3 bonus to the wearer's Intelligence Ability score.
4 - Jaguar Ring
The fast and beautiful jaguars of east Vulcor are often hunted for their leg bones that are used in these charms. A Jaguar Ring adds +2 to the wearer's Speed Ability score.
5 - Minor Relic
Minor Relics are given to initiates of the holy temples but somehow a lot of these charms have found their way to other users. The charms increases the Willpower Ability score of the wearer by +3.
6 - Ox Charm
An Ox Charm is a small bone amulet carved to resemble an ox or a bull. The primitive magic that is imbued within such charms adds a +1 bonus to the Toughness Ability and derives from the savage shamans that live in Vulcor.
7 - Luck Ring
Luck Rings are simple charms made by old gypsy fortune tellers. When worn the charm may warn the wearer of imminent danger and as a result a +3 bonus is added to the effect of Defence skills.
8 - Mage Eye
A Mage Eye is actually a small amber orb that resembles an eye. Such a charm is bestowed with simple magic that adds +10 to the damage effect of distance spells.
9 - Harm Stone
Harm Stones are made from the sharp rocks that are found everywhere in Vulcor. The rock is imbued with magic and when strapped to a melee weapon of any kind the charm adds +12 to the damage effect.
10 - Life Pearl
From the depths of the Sterling Lake in Blissmor come these magic pearls that are highly sought for their ability to increase the effect of healing spells by +8.
11 - Archer Token
These charms are crafted by the forest people of Mystor and the secrets of the process are closely guarded. The feathered Archer Tokens adds +10 to the damage effect of missile weapons.
12 - Mage Claw
Mage Claws are sharp metal claws that are fitted to the back of a hand and act like catalysts for close ranged Bane spells. The claws add +12 to the damage effect of touch spells.
13 - Eagle Eye
The desiccated eyes of an eagle is one of the ingredients used in this type of charm, which has earned it its name. The charms add +3 to the accuracy effect of missile weapons.
14 - Air Boots
When donning these feathered boots the wearer can make long and high jump, making travel on foot much easier. As a bonus the boots add +3 to the avoid effect of spells and skills.
15 - Cleric Cross
When held forward towards an enemy this powerful cross will add a +3 bonus to the bearer's spell resist effect of spells and skills.
16 - Ice Skin
This relatively small charm produces a kind of thin, icy membrane around the bearer. The membrane acts more or less like a suit of light armour, providing a +12 bonus to damage reduction of spells and skills.
17 - Time Orb
This fist-sized magic orb that you found on Shirard radiates a faint purple light. You realise that your reactions improve noticeably while holding the orb!
