Spells:

Introduction
The list of spells below is divided into the Town products (Orumant, Dagonar and Ravarcil) so that you can easily see where you can buy them. Spells are divided into 2 main categories, namely those based on the Intelligence Ability and those based on Willpower. Furthermore, there are 4 sub-categories: Spectral, Bane, Guardian and Spirit. Spells that fall within the same sun-category are typically related in some way. For instance, all spells that deal instant damage are found in the Bane category.
ORUMANT
1 - Blink (spectral)
Blink is the most unstable spell in the line of translocations spells. Because of the speed with which this spell is cast it may sometimes fizzle - mainly because the caster messes up with the incantation. However, when it works it will teleport the caster to a random location in the Arena.
2 - Shift (spectral)
Unsatisfied with the somewhat unstable Blink spell the masters of the Tower of Orumant invented this spell, which is just an upgraded version. This spell will fizzle less but the caster still has no influence on where he will be teleported to.
3 - Ghost Walk (spectral)
Now this is more like it! By making use of the Ghost Walk the caster may teleport to a location that he selects himself - so no more random teleportation here! The downside of the spell, though, is that there is a slight chance that it will fizzle.
4 - Wraith Walk (spectral)
Just like Ghost Walking this spell will allow the caster to magically move to a different location in a matter of seconds. This spell is even better than the sister version in that it will not fizzle as often as Ghost Walk.
5 - Zap (bane)
The Zap spell is the most elemental Bane spell that is taught in the magic Towers in the towns and cities of Eidolon. The spell releases a ray of raw energy that the caster accumulates when speaking the incantation.
6 - Double Zap (bane)
Once the old masters of the Towers learned how to harness the raw energy of a basic Zap spell they soon learned to increase the power. The result was the Double Zap spell that releases two rays instead of one.
7 - Hand of Pain (bane)
The force of this close range spell is that it will often take the opponent by surprise. The reason for this is that it isn't that obvious that a spell is being cast - it simply looks like the caster is trying to grab the opponent. However, when the Hand of Pain touches a person it will drain a small amount of Life from the victim.
8 - Big Zap (bane)
This is the most powerful version of the spell category that is based upon releasing raw energy. Unlike the Double Zap spell the Big Zap is just one large bolt of energy that is so powerful that it will even melt soft metals.
9 - Ice Kiss (bane)
When someone tries to kiss you, you would hardly suspect that they mean to harm you. This spell exploits this as it delivers a powerful draining effect through a kiss, often rendering the victim numb or slain on the ground. Off course, using the spell in the Arena may often give the opponent a clue as to your intentions.
10 - Sticky Fire (bane)
Having seen the impressive effect of the varios fire bomb types the masters of the Towers soon realised that a spell with similar effects would be very potent indeed. The first result in the line of fire spells is this spell, which engulfs the victim in sticky, burning plasma.
11 - Clawkill (bane)
When using this spell the caster's main hand transforms into a large, sharp claw. If used against an opponent like a melee weapon the claw will cause great damage.
12 - Inferno (bane)
Inferno is a much favoured spell by those who fight in the Arenas. The spell was invented by a crazy wizard of Vulcor who ended his days in prison accused for many accounts of arson. Rumour has it that the wizard said that he was communicating with dragons and that the reptilians would one day free him...obviously they never did.
13 - Drainhand (bane)
This spell is particularly useful. Not only will the Drainhand cause damage in victims but the damage taken will also be transferred to heal the caster! This means that the spell is effectively two in one.
14 - Lightning (bane)
It may not come as SHOCKING news to you that Lightnings are quite dangerous. With this spell you have the advantage of being able to blast your opponent away from a safe distance.
15 - Fireball (bane)
As the name implies this spell produces a deadly Fireball that explodes when it hits its target.
16 - Lethal Claw (bane)
Lethal Claw is a much more potent version of the Clawkill spell. One of the main ingredients is a claw from one of the huge trolls that roam the wastes of Vulcor. Unfortunately the claw must be taken from a living troll, which makes it much harder to come by.
17 - Magic Coat (guardian)
This spell creates a shimmering force around the caster's body that acts more or less like a suit of body. The force will absorb a small amount of damage caused by weapons, thus offering the caster slightly better protection versus attacks.
18 - Blur Body (guardian)
This Guardian spell improves the caster's chance of avoiding getting hit by attacks made with weapons by partly transferring the caster's body to another plane of existence. The spell will stack with defence skills if used in the same turn.
19 - Mind Block (guardian)
Mind Block is a spell that the former head master of the Tower of Orumant invented as a means to improve magic resistance. Ironically he died in a horrible explosion that was caused by a Fireball spell that somehow went wrong.
20 - Runic Vest (guardian)
This spell works on the same principles as Magic Coat but the force surrounding the caster is slightly stronger and will absorb more damage.
21 - Ghost Body (guardian)
Inspired by an encounter with a ghostly humanoid shape near one of the stone circles in the deep forest of Mystor, one of the master of the Tower in Ravarcil invented this spell. The spell makes the caster's body appear semi transparent.
22 - Brain Shield (guardian)
This spell creates a magical barrier around the caster's brain that effectively increases the chances of resisting spells. The barrier creates a slight tingle, but not so much as to become unbearable.
23 - Spell Armour (guardian)
With this spell the effect of heavy armour is more or less duplicated. However, since the spell is based on a force field it should only be used in fights as it may also prevent other things than weapons to get to the caster...like a keg of beer!
24 - Celestial Body (guardian)
Under the influence of a Celestial Body spell the caster becomes ethereal for a short period of time, making it harder for opponents to hit with weapons.
25 - Mental Wall (guardian)
Unlike the Brain Shield this spell creates a big, invisible magic wall around the caster. The wall acts as a obstacle for spells and will make it easier for the caster to resist hostile spells.
26 - Stoneskin (guardian)
Even today it is unknown who invented this spell that is an improved version of Spell Armour. Some say that it must have been a former master of the Tower of Dagonar while others claim that its origins can be traced back to Mystor. No matter what, the spell offers great protection against enemy attacks!
27 - Blurry (guardian)
Avoiding being hit becomes so much easier if this spell is used. The caster's body will appear as a blurry form to the enemy making it harder to direct attacks at the right places.
28 - Minor Cure (spirit)
By the use of this spell a number of magical bandages appear and they will automatically wrap themselves around any wound the caster may have suffered. Of course, the spell is only effective against small wounds and scratches!
29 - Medium Cure (spirit)
As an improved version of Minor Cure this spells allows for greater healing and may even be used to re-attach small body parts like toes and fingers.
30 - Strong Cure (spirit)
This spell is said to be based on the same type of divine magic that the queens have enchanted the Arenas with so that heroes cannot die while training. If this is true it is obviously far less potent than the magic used by the queens.
31 - Power Cure (spirit)
Conjuring a vortex of energy from nearby plants and elements, this spell can potentially heal even gaping wounds. The visual effects of the spell are just as spectacular as magical bandages that look like bands of shimmering light are wrapped around the caster's wounds.
32 - Repair Wound (spirit)
Even though the Heal Wound spell is more difficult to cast, the healing potential of this spell exceeds it slightly. The reason that Repair Wound is easier to learn and does not require as much willpower to control is that the result is somewhat random.
33 - Heal Wound (spirit)
With this spell the caster will know just how much he can count on being healed. Ever since the spell was invented by a priest of Mystor it has saved many a hero from dying on the battlefield.
34 - Super Heal (spirit)
This spell is even better than the Heal Wound spell and though it could possibly turn the tide of great battles only few ever get to cast it because it requires so much willpower to control the divine force that is needed.
DAGONAR
1 - Lower Will (spectral)
This spell is made for penetrating the opponent's spell resistance so that the spells that follow, like a nasty Fireball, don't go to waste being resisted.
2 - Evil Eye (spectral)
The Evil Eye will cause the foe to miss more often than usual
3 - Plague (spectral)
Cast Plague on your foe and his attacks will cause less damage.
4 - Dispel Magic (spectral)
Dispel Magic is one of those spells that have a nasty habit of fizzling in just the wrong moment, namely when you most need it to work. When the spell actually works, which is most of the time after all, it cancels Invisibility and Holy Aura.
5 - Attraction (spectral)
If you are confident in your own close quarter abilities then you should really employ this spell as it will draw your opponent closer. This may interrupt his own move actions, causing an otherwise great battle plan to crumble!
6 - Confusion (spectral)
If your foe tries to move under the influence of this spell he will not be able to control where to go and will thus move in random directions.
7 - Fear (spectral)
It's about time your foes fear you properly! They will litterally run away from you now.
8 - Construct (spectral)
Tired of all the destruction? Then use this spell to increase the size of obstacles.
9 - Ice Field (bane)
You must select a field for this spell to target but the damage caused is tremendous. Note that it still requires line of sight!
10 - Paralyze (bane)
Your foe will move slower than normal when you use this spell.
11 - Fire Dart (bane)
Fry your foe from within with this Fire Dart and watch him dance in pain.
12 - Spell Stop (bane)
The foe's first spell will be cancelled. That'll teach those wizards a lesson!
13 - Poison Acid (bane)
Here's a nasty trick as the foe will suffer right away but also at the end of the turn.
14 - Fire Field (bane)
Select a target field and watch your foe be toasted by the roaring fire in the blast.
15 - Ghosthand (bane)
This spell will ignore some of the foe's armour and almost go right through.
16 - Invisibility (guardian)
You will be invisible for the rest of the turn unless you do anything else but Move.
17 - Baneguard (guardian)
This is a good way to increase your chances of resisting spells.
18 - Reflect pain (guardian)
If this spell doesn't fizzle the damage caused by the foe's next weapon attack is reflected.
19 - Mental Armour (guardian)
This reduces the damage of spells cast at you so it's almost like armour.
20 - Spell Block (guardian)
This spell will block the foe's next spell. But know that this spell may fizzle.
21 - Vanish (guardian)
You will be almost 100% safe from the foe's targeting for as long as you only move.
22 - Reflect Spell (guardian)
Use this spell to deflect the foe's Bane spells so it will harm him instead of you.
23 - Holy Aura (guardian)
You will be totally immune to damage 2 times if this spell doesn't fizzle!
24 - Neutralize (spirit)
In addition to healing you a bit this spell makes you immune to Poison Acid.
25 - Bless (spirit)
Holy guidance leads your hand and your attacks will cause more damage.
26 - Rain of Life (spirit)
This provides instant healing as well as at the end of the turn but is a rather slow spell.
27 - Bravery (spirit)
This spell counters the effects of the Fear, Confusion and Attraction spells.
28 - Mystic Hand (spirit)
Your attacks will hit much better when they are guided by this spell.
